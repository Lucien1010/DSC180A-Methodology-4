**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

I found the history of digital money and how it developed into blockchain especially interesting. It is fascinating how people approached the problem of making transactions without depending on one central authority. 
Smart contracts take this further by enforcing agreed rules, such as holding a deposit until certain conditions are met. Although they still depend on correct code, I think this creates interesting ways to make transactions more secure. 

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

We would like to replicate and improve the previous project "Vera." The idea is to use a verified university degree as an anchor and connect other credentials to the same holder, while showing which are verified or self-reported. 
Beyond building systems, we would like to compare its verification time, cost, and trust requirements with a regular server that issues signed documents.

**3. What is a potential change you’d make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One potential change is to combine Vera v.2 with another previous project "Analyst Scorecard," which uses escrow deposits and records analysts' predictions and scores on-chain. 
Vera could connect an analyst's credentials to the wallet used for their predictions. Their profile could then show both their qualifications and their prediction history. 
This would let someone evaluate an analyst using actual performance alongside credentials, rather than relying only on what the analyst claims.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in using property-based testing to check different sequences of credential registration and revocation. If we add "Analyst Scorecard," we could also simulate analysts with different skill levels to see whether the scores distinguish skill from luck. 
Measuring transaction costs and verification time would help us evaluate whether the combined system is practical.
